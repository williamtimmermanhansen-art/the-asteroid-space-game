v16 - COMMUNITY UPDATE
===============================================================================
- Increased playable map size.
- Increased asteroid population and spawn frequency.
- Magazine capacities increased by 15 shots:
  - 45 starting shots
  - 75 shots after first Magazine upgrade
  - 140 shots after second Magazine upgrade
- Magazine upgrades continue to increase bullet power:
  - x1.0 starting power
  - x1.5 with 75-shot Magazine
  - x2.0 with 140-shot Magazine
- Added dedicated Reload System upgrade path:
  - 3.0 sec -> 2.5 sec: 75 credits
  - 2.5 sec -> 2.0 sec: 125 credits
  - 2.0 sec -> 1.5 sec: 200 credits
  - 1.5 sec -> 1.0 sec: 300 credits
  - 1.0 sec -> 0.5 sec: 450 credits
- Automatic empty-magazine reload uses the upgraded reload time.
- Added purchasable Deep Space background:
  - 250-credit one-time unlock
  - free switching between Standard and Deep Space after purchase
  - background choice saved with SAVE CODE
- Added supplied Deep Space image as game asset.
- Added supplied music track "A few jumps away" by Arthur Vyncke.
- Music configured to loop.
- Added music attribution/license notice to README.
- Added progressively more complex ship silhouettes at higher Space Ranks,
  inspired by the supplied ship-design references.
- Added very subtle "Nortic Pixel Studio" branding at the bottom of the game.
- Added start-screen developer credit:
  "Developed by William T. Hansen of Nortic Pixel Studio, with assistance from ChatGPT."
- Preserved English-only in-game UI.

===============================================================================
v15 - HEART LIMIT AT RANK #10
===============================================================================
- Extra-heart purchase opportunities limited to:
  - Space Rank #5: Space Colonel
  - Space Rank #10: Space Marshal
- No new heart purchase opportunities after Rank #10.
- Each extra heart costs 150 credits.
- Maximum of 2 extra hearts per run.
- Heart purchases are stored in the active SAVE CODE.

===============================================================================
v14 - EXTRA HEART SYSTEM
===============================================================================
- Introduced purchasable extra hearts.
- Cost: 150 credits per heart.
- Initial design allowed one opportunity every 5 Space Ranks.
- Heart purchases added one current life and one extra life slot.
- Lost hearts were not automatically restored.
- Heart purchases were made persistent in SAVE CODE data.
- Later refined in v15 to stop after Rank #10.

CURRENT DESIGN / CORE SYSTEMS
===============================================================================
- PC, iOS/mobile, and Android browser support.
- PC controls include WASD/arrows, Space, R, M, I, O, ESC, and zoom controls.
- Touch controls for mobile.
- SAVE CODE persistence.
- Automatic Space Rank progression up to Space God at 1,000 asteroids.
- Purchasable Ship Titles:
  - King
  - Master
  - Legend
- Title credit multipliers and full-set x11 multiplier.
- Red Elite Asteroids appear periodically and award x2 asteroid credits.
- Every 10 destroyed asteroids grants a base 15-credit bonus before active title multiplier.
- Gun, Engine, Shield, Magazine, Reload, Ship Color, Heart, Background,
  audio, and visual-progression systems.
