THE ASTEROID SPACE GAME

Destroy asteroids, earn credits, climb the Space Ranks, and upgrade your ship.

PC: W/A/S/D or Arrow Keys to fly · Space to fire · R to reload · M to open Ship Systems · +/- or mouse wheel to zoom
Mobile / Touchscreen: Use the on-screen controls, Reload, and the +/- zoom buttons.
Elite asteroid: Every 5th asteroid is red, has more health, and gives 2× credits.
Audio: Space ambience and calm indie music begin after START / LOAD.

Enter your own code or generate one. Saves stay on this browser/device.

iPhone tip: Add the game to your Home Screen for the most app-like full-screen experience.

ℹ️ GAME INFO

Controls, ranks, upgrades, rewards, and elite asteroids.

⌨️ PC CONTROLS

  • W / Arrow Up — Thrust forward
  • S / Arrow Down — Brake
  • A / Arrow Left — Turn left
  • D / Arrow Right — Turn right
  • Space — Fire
  • R — Manual reload
  • M — Open / close Ship Systems
  • I — Open / close Game Info
  • O — Open / close Settings
  • + / - — Zoom in / out
  • Mouse wheel — Zoom in / out
  • Esc — Close Ship Systems, Settings, or Game Info

These ranks are earned automatically by destroying asteroids. Extra-heart purchases unlock at rank #5 (Space Colonel) and #10 (Space Marshal). Rank #10 is the final heart opportunity.

❤️ EXTRA HEARTS

  • An extra heart costs 150 credits.
  • One purchase opportunity unlocks at Space Rank #5 and another at Space Rank #10.
  • Rank #10 is the final heart opportunity. No more heart purchases unlock after it.
  • Each opportunity can only be used once, for a maximum of 2 extra hearts per run.
  • Buying a heart adds one current life and one extra life slot. Previously lost hearts are not restored.

🎨 SHIP COLORS

  • Red, Green, Orange, Purple, or Pink — 25 credits per color change.
  • Reset to Normal — 30 credits.
  • Ship colors only change the hull appearance. Space Ranks and Ship Title bonuses stay active.
  • Normal uses the default appearance of your current Ship Title.

👑 SHIP TITLES · PURCHASED IN ORDER

  • King · 100 credits — Gold ship and ×2 credits
  • Master · 700 credits — Light-blue ship and ×3 credits
  • Legend · 1500 credits — Legend design and ×5 title bonus
  • Full title set — Owning King + Master + Legend activates ×11 credits

🛠️ UPGRADES

  • Weapons — Faster firing as levels increase. Level 2+ adds alternating side cannons.
  • Magazine — Starts at 30 shots. Upgrade to 60 shots for 100 credits, then 125 shots for 350 credits. Each Magazine upgrade also boosts bullet damage by +50%.
  • Engine — Increases thrust, maximum speed, and turning speed.
  • Shield — Adds 30 maximum shield per level and improves shield regeneration.
  • Reload — Manual reload takes 3 seconds. An empty magazine triggers a 3-second automatic reload.

🌌 DEEP SPACE BACKGROUND

  • Distant star streaks drift far across the background.
  • A faint wormhole can be seen deep in space.
  • These are atmosphere effects and do not change gameplay.

🔴 RED ELITE ASTEROIDS

  • Every 5th spawned asteroid is a red elite asteroid.
  • Red asteroids have more health and need more hits to destroy.
  • They pay ×2 normal asteroid credits.
  • The elite ×2 reward stacks with your active Ship Title credit multiplier.

💳 CREDIT REWARDS

  • Destroy asteroids to earn credits.
  • Every 10 destroyed asteroids gives a 15-credit bonus before the active title multiplier.
  • Use credits in Ship Systems for upgrades and Ship Titles.

💾 SAVE CODE

  • Use a personal 4- or 5-digit code to continue an active run.
  • The run autosaves on the same browser and device.
  • If you reach Game Over, the active run for that code is deleted.
